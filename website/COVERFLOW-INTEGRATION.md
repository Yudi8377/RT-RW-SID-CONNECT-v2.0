# Smart Village 3D Coverflow integration

Adds a responsive, keyboard- and touch-operable 3D feature carousel to `website/index.html`, themed for RT/RW CONNECT. The existing landing page, Supabase runtime, action bridge, news, trial, and dashboard links are retained.

- `coverflow.css`: isolated component styling; respects reduced-motion.
- `coverflow.js`: local vanilla-JS controls, dots, keyboard navigation, and touch swipes.
- The gallery is a feature showcase, not a claim that every listed service is live.
- Photography is loaded from Unsplash image URLs at runtime; replace with approved project-owned images before production if required.
- Template reference: https://templatemo.com/live/templatemo_595_3d_coverflow
- TemplateMo states its templates may be modified and used for commercial and non-commercial sites: https://templatemo.com/tm-595-3d-coverflow
