# RT/RW-SID CONNECT — Support Policy v1.0

Status: DESIGN

## Support levels

### Community
Documentation and community/self-service guidance. No guaranteed response-time SLA.

### Pro
Product support within published scope, installation guidance, upgrade guidance, and issue triage.

### Institution / Partner
Contract-defined support, deployment assistance, training where included, optional SLA, and optional migration/integration services.

## Paid services

Services may include installation, configuration, training, migration, backup strategy, integration, customization, and managed deployment. A service fee is not a donation.

## Data safety

Support processes must never require unnecessary disclosure of citizen data. Troubleshooting should prefer sanitized diagnostics and least-privilege access.
