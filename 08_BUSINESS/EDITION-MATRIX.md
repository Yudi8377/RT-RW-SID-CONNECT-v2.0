## Trial behavior

The Pro Trial is a feature entitlement, not a data-destructive mode. On expiry, local data remains intact, Community capabilities remain available, and Pro-only capabilities become unavailable until licensed.

## License states

Suggested states: COMMUNITY, TRIAL_ACTIVE, TRIAL_EXPIRED, PRO_ACTIVE, INSTITUTION_ACTIVE, PARTNER_MANAGED, SUSPENDED_FOR_ADMINISTRATIVE_REASON.

Do not implement entitlement solely as a client-editable boolean.
