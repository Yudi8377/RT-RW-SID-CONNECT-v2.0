# RT/RW-SID CONNECT — Community Policy v1.0

Status: DESIGN

## Community promise

The Community edition exists to keep basic RT/RW/SID administration accessible to communities that cannot or do not wish to purchase a professional plan.

## Community baseline

The baseline may include resident/community records, RT/RW verification workflow, basic letters/documents, events, offline local data, offline queue, sync capability, backup/restore, basic audit trail, and basic reports. The exact boundary is controlled by the Edition Matrix.

## Fair access

No user should be pressured, shamed or misled into paying. The product should clearly distinguish free Community access, voluntary contribution, time-limited Trial, paid License, and paid Services.

## Data continuity

Trial expiry must not destroy the user's local data. A user should retain access to the Community feature set where technically supported.

## Governance

Changes to the Community promise should be documented and versioned. Breaking changes should not be silently introduced through an installer update.
