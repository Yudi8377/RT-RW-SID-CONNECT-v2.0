# RT/RW-SID CONNECT — Contribution & Donation Policy v1.0

Status: DESIGN

## Definition

A contribution/donation is voluntary support for continued development and community availability. It does not automatically purchase a license, feature entitlement, SLA, installation service or customization.

## Potential channels

QRIS, bank transfer, GitHub Sponsors, and a future dedicated support portal. Each official collection channel must be legally and operationally reviewed before presentation in the product.

## Suggested UX

Dukung RT/RW-SID CONNECT. Aplikasi ini dikembangkan agar teknologi administrasi masyarakat tetap terjangkau. Jika aplikasi bermanfaat, Anda dapat membantu keberlanjutan pengembangan.

Presets: Rp25.000, Rp50.000, Rp100.000, Rp250.000, Rp500.000, Nominal lain. Frequency: sekali or bulanan where supported.

## Separation from licensing

Never label a mandatory Pro payment as a donation. If the payer receives a defined entitlement, use License, Subscription, Service or Sponsorship Agreement as appropriate.

## Transparency

Any percentage allocation displayed in the product must reflect actual bookkeeping. Example percentages are design placeholders only and must not be presented as factual spending allocations until verified.
