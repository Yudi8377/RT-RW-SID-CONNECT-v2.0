# RT/RW-SID CONNECT — Business Model v1.0

Status: DESIGN / NOT YET COMMERCIAL PRICING
Release impact: None by itself
Core principle: Community access to basic civic administration must not depend on payment.

## Product model

One core engine with multiple models: Community (Rp0), Pro Trial, Pro, Institution, Partner, and voluntary Community Support.

## What is monetized

Revenue may come from professional features, organization licensing, implementation, deployment, migration, training, support, maintenance, integrations/customization, sponsorship, and voluntary contributions.

The project must not monetize by withholding a user's own basic civic records or making data inaccessible after a trial expires.

## Sustainability

Commercial users and service customers can cross-subsidize continued Community availability, security, documentation, maintenance and community support.

## Pricing

Pricing is intentionally NOT final in v2.0.1 technical release work. Any price must be approved after measuring support cost, deployment effort, feature scope, organization size, user/device scope, infrastructure cost, security/maintenance obligations, and Indonesian tax/legal requirements.

## Terminology

Use Community, Trial, License, Support, Sponsorship, Contribution, and Services. Do not describe a paid service as a donation when the payer receives a contractual entitlement.

## Core Social Contract

> RT/RW-SID CONNECT will not intentionally disable basic civic administration merely because an organization cannot pay.

The business layer should increase sustainability without undermining the project's community mission.
