# RT/RW–SID CONNECT — Civic Program Catalog and Regulatory Review Map

Status: planning catalog. It is not legal advice, not a complete legal register and not evidence that any proposed module is implemented or formally endorsed by a government institution.

## 1. Catalog principles

Each program must have a defined public purpose, accountable owner, authorized scope, budget/funding assumption, operational workflow, data owner, privacy classification, outcome indicators and review process. A public-sector relationship or national mandate must not be claimed until formally documented.

Use the following legal workflow before launch:
1. Identify the applicable national law, implementing regulation, ministerial rules and local regulation.
2. Verify the current text and status from an authoritative source (official JDIH, BPK database, regulator or issuing authority).
3. Record amendments, revocations, effective dates, geographic scope and responsible authority.
4. Ask an authorized legal/policy reviewer to resolve ambiguity.
5. Map the verified requirement to feature, data fields, access controls, retention, process and acceptance tests.
6. Review on source update and on a scheduled basis.

## 3. Regulatory/source families to verify per module

This is a source checklist, not a final opinion on applicability or current validity.

- Personal data: UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 on Personal Data Protection; check implementing rules and sector-specific obligations.
- Public information: UU No. 14 Tahun 2008 on Public Information Disclosure; verify implementing regulations and the applicable public-information exemption process.
- Public services and complaints: UU No. 25 Tahun 2009 on Public Services and current implementing rules.
- Electronic systems and transactions: UU ITE and its amendments, PP No. 71 Tahun 2019 and current implementing rules; verify obligations based on system/operator classification.
- SPBE and architecture: Perpres No. 95 Tahun 2018 and Perpres No. 132 Tahun 2022; applicability depends on whether the operator/service is within scope.
- One Data Indonesia: Perpres No. 39 Tahun 2019; apply appropriate standards, metadata and exchange governance where relevant.
- Village governance and village programs: current consolidated village law, implementing regulations, ministerial rules and local regulations. Check amendments rather than relying on an old copy.
- Cooperatives: current cooperative law, amendments, implementing rules and licensing/sector obligations.
- Health: Health Law and current implementing regulations, professional standards, medical-record and electronic-system requirements as applicable.
- Disaster management: UU No. 24 Tahun 2007 and current implementing rules.
- Food security: UU No. 18 Tahun 2012 and applicable amendments/implementing rules.
- Disability inclusion: UU No. 8 Tahun 2016 and applicable implementing rules.
- Education/child protection: relevant education, child-protection, accessibility and personal-data rules for the audience and service.
- Journalism: applicable press law, press-council standards and editorial ethics; distinguish journalism, government communication, opinion, advertising and sponsored content.
- Copyright/culture: applicable copyright, cultural heritage, community rights and consent rules.
- Employment: applicable labor, placement, apprenticeship, privacy and anti-scam rules.

Official verification starting points:
- BPK regulations database: https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/
- National legislation database/JDIH: https://jdihn.go.id/
- Ministry/regulator official JDIH portals and official regional JDIH.
- Press Council: https://dewanpers.or.id/
- Official system/vendor documentation for technical and dependency changes.

Before any release, record the exact authoritative document URL and verification date. A generic portal homepage is a discovery starting point, not sufficient evidence for a specific legal conclusion.

## 4. AI controls by program type

- News: AI may assist source discovery, classification, summarization and privacy flags; a human editor makes publication decisions and handles corrections/right of reply.
- Legal literacy: show official source, jurisdiction and effective-status date; clearly distinguish general information from case-specific advice.
- Health: provide approved general education and service navigation; do not diagnose, prescribe or expose personal medical records.
- Social assistance: help explain published rules and prepare checklists; do not make final eligibility or exclusion decisions.
- Cooperative: may analyze approved aggregate figures; no autonomous financial transactions or member decisions.
- Good-deed recognition: recognize specific opt-in verified contributions; never build a permanent moral score or infer character from personal data.
- Education/employment: assist learning and skill matching; do not guarantee credentials or employment.
- Disaster: relay verified official advisories and preparedness guidance; show source/time and defer emergency instructions to authorized sources.
- Public data: aggregate and redact; do not infer that a field is public merely because it is technically accessible.

## 5. Program proposal template

Every new module must complete:
- Module ID/name and public purpose.
- Owning organization and accountable role.
- Jurisdiction and territory scope.
- Intended users and accessibility requirements.
- Workflow, lifecycle and human decision points.
- Data inventory, source, classification, lawful basis/purpose and retention.
- Data controller/processor and sharing agreements where applicable.
- Tables/API/storage needs and relationships to canonical IDs.
- RLS policy design plus positive and negative authorization tests.
- AI agent tools, memory boundaries and evaluation suite.
- Public fields, redaction rules and publication approval.
- KPI definitions and evidence sources.
- Budget/cost model and operational support.
- Regulatory sources, verification date and reviewer.
- Threat model, incident response, rollback and release acceptance.

## 6. Initial rollout order

1. Peta Program registry and indicator model.
2. Jurnalisme Warga, transparency and editorial safeguards.
3. Education, youth skills and verified opportunity directory.
4. Public aspirations/complaints and local service follow-up.
5. Tourism, culture, local economy and cooperative information.
6. Environment, food resilience and disaster preparedness.
7. Social wellbeing, health referral and accessibility after data-governance review.
8. Shared Regulatory Intelligence Center and advanced AI automation after source validation, evaluation and audit foundations are tested.

This order is a proposed implementation sequence, not a ranking of the social value of each program.

## 7. Success criteria

- Each program has an accountable owner and approved data map.
- Every legal claim points to a current authoritative source and verification date.
- No private or sensitive records leak into public dashboards or shared AI knowledge.
- Users can see a service/program status and how it was verified.
- Complaints and editorial corrections have traceable workflows.
- AI suggestions are measurable and reviewable; humans retain reserved decisions.
- Costs, latency, accessibility, security and actual outcomes are monitored.
